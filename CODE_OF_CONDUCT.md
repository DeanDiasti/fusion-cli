# Community code of conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

- Be respectful and explain technical disagreements with evidence.
- Give useful, specific feedback and assume good intent.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate or share another person's private data.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.
- Credit other people's work and respect their licenses.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close disruptive
threads, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate and protect
the people affected. Conduct involving sensitive information can be reported
privately through GitHub's private reporting channel rather than a public issue.
